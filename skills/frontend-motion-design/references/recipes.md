# Scene recipes

Proven combinations for video scenes (faceless/documentary, explainers, promos). Sync each effect to the narration word it illustrates.

| Scene | Combination | Example prompt |
|---|---|---|
| Opening hook | word reveal (15) + punch zoom (22) on the strongest word | "reveal the hook word by word and punch zoom on 'vanished'" |
| Date or era | mask reveal (20) on the year + blur in (05) on the subtitle | "mask reveal 1969 and blur in the subtitle" |
| Big number | count-up (14) + punch zoom (22) when it stops | "count up to 8 million and punch zoom when it lands" |
| Map and journey | draw-on (19) on the route + pulse (12) on the destination | "draw the route on the map and pulse the arrival point" |
| Before and after | wipe (21) | "show the old and new photo with a wipe" |
| Data over time | bar grow (23) + stagger (06) | "bars per decade growing in a stagger" |
| Quote or document | typewriter (13) + highlight (17) on the key passage | "type the quote and highlight the main line" |
| Video topics | stagger (06) + slide in (02) | "the 5 topics stagger in, sliding from the left" |
| Chapter change | wipe (21) or mask reveal (20) | "change chapter with a wipe and mask reveal the title" |
| End screen (subscribe) | pop in (03) + pulse (12) | "pop in the subscribe button and keep it pulsing" |
| Still photo | Ken Burns (~5% over 6s, `sine.inOut`) | "slow Ken Burns on the photo" |
